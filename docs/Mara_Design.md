# Mara Love Interest: pet-role dialogue design (v0.1.14)

## Tone update

v0.1.14 shifts Mara's voice in the established Handler/Pet arrangement from firm-but-patient to cold, impatient, and openly dismissive. She treats routine obedience as the bare minimum, gives short commands, criticises hesitation, and occasionally calls the player a useless or hopeless pet.

The new tone applies to:
- The second pet confirmation, collar inspection, and house rules.
- House entry and the pet-room hub.
- Cushion waiting, random pet orders, bowl meals, and cage access.
- Training introduction, random lesson instructions, completion, and stop dialogue.
- Optional housework while in pet mode.
- Discipline-room offer, tool choice, frame scene, completion, decline, and stop dialogue.

Ordinary shop, cafe, romance, and non-pet house interactions retain their previous warmer dialogue. This keeps Mara's harsher speech tied to the agreed roleplay context rather than changing her entire character everywhere.

## Mechanics and save compatibility

No mechanics or progression values changed in v0.1.14. Existing activity chance rolls, time costs, stat rewards, stop/decline options, and daily flags are preserved. No new save fields are introduced; objectVersion.mara remains 9.
